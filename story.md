# The world: NextVoice vs Gallantir

> Everything here is fictional. No real people, companies, messages or records
> are involved. The world is a frame to make the security concepts concrete and
> a little more fun to read.

## The two sides

**NextVoice** is a youth-rights organisation. It campaigns against institutions
buying systems that profile how young people take part in politics. Off the
clock, some of its members unwind together in an MMO guild called **NextVoice
Nightwatch** and a community chat server. The guild is a social space, not the
organisation's membership list.

**Gallantir** is an intelligence contractor. It sells a platform called
**CivicLens** and markets itself as a protector of communities. Several of
Gallantir's prospective customers have paused their purchases after hearing
NextVoice's concerns. Gallantir's leadership wants those customers back, and it
wants advance sight of NextVoice's unpublished arguments before a procurement
hearing.

## The inciting incident

Tonight the guild is about to raid a fortress called the Watchtower. A member
drops a bombshell: a Gallantir **sales slide**, forwarded by a buyer, quotes a
**private** guild joke word-for-word.

> "If the Watchtower boss taxes our snacks again, we are unionising the dragons."

The slide labels it a "community risk indicator." The channel was private. So how
did Gallantir get it? That question is the thread the whole project pulls on.

## The investigation, as a security story

Pulling the thread turns up a chain of ordinary-looking failures: a leaky
permission check, a data feed that should have been switched off, then a handful
of classic attacks a surveillance operation might lean on. Rather than one person
poking at it by hand, **an AI agent swarm works the case.** Red agents show how
each attack would land. Blue agents show how to see it and stop it. A human
directs the rounds and decides what to trust.

The goal is not to "win." It is to understand, concretely, with real evidence
where the lab is real and clearly-labelled analysis where it is not, how these
attacks work and how you defend against them.

## The honest ending

One flaw gets genuinely fixed and proven fixed (see
[`dossiers/01-broken-access-control.md`](dossiers/01-broken-access-control.md)).
The broader threats are studied, not weaponised. Gallantir is not destroyed.
Collection from the guild stops, one sale pauses and the wider struggle
continues. That is the realistic shape of defensive security work, and it is the
shape this project keeps.
