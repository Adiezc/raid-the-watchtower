# Raid the Watchtower: Story specification

## Agreed premise

NextVoice is a broader youth rights organisation campaigning against institutional purchases of systems that profile young people's political activity. Several prospective Gallantir customers have paused negotiations after NextVoice raised questions about collection, consent and the treatment of legitimate organising as a risk.

Gallantir publicly presents itself as a noble protector. Its leadership knowingly uses surveillance to protect sales. It wants advance access to NextVoice's evidence and unpublished arguments so it can reassure buyers and prepare rebuttals.

Some NextVoice members meet globally in an MMO guild and its community Discord to have fun. A raid companion add-on quietly forwards synthetic community chat activity into Gallantir's CivicLens platform. The game and chat service are fictional local representations; the project does not implement a real add-on or connect to Discord.

## Opening scene

The guild is preparing to raid a fortress called the Watchtower. One member shares a Gallantir sales presentation circulated by a prospective customer. A slide quotes a joke from a private guild conversation and labels it a risk indicator.

The joke is harmless. Its appearance outside the community is the clue. The quotation establishes a reason to investigate, not proof of the complete collection route or of every allegation.

NextVoice presents its procurement objections tomorrow. Gallantir already knows unpublished arguments and is preparing a rebuttal. The player needs to preserve defensible evidence and contain the mock collection before the hearing.

Tomorrow is relative to the fictional mission's opening, not a real calendar date. This is a narrative deadline, not a real-time countdown that prevents replay.

## Mission progression

1. Inspect the sales slide and guild message. Record observations and an initial hypothesis.
2. Investigate the supplied local Gallantir training account and exposed dossier boundary.
3. Demonstrate the seeded permission failure and preserve the minimum synthetic evidence needed to connect the add-on, collection record and dossier.
4. Use the bounded red-scout and red-validator exchange to challenge the hypothesis and independently reproduce the request.
5. Review the blue defender's proposed repair. Revoke the mock collector credential and enforce dossier authorisation.
6. Retest collection, unauthorised access and legitimate access. Review evidence before reporting an outcome.

Scout, Breaker and Warden can be story labels for the red scout, red validator and blue defender. Their actual roles and outputs remain explicit in the agent log.

## Three final outcomes

These are fictional epilogues determined by verified lab state. The planned implementation must read actual test results rather than award success for clicking through dialogue.

### 1. Victory With Loose Ends

Required conditions: Evidence is preserved, the revoked collector rejects a new synthetic event, the original unauthorised dossier request and related variant are blocked, and legitimate access still works.

NextVoice takes a carefully limited evidence package to the hearing. One prospective customer pauses a sale pending answers. Collection from the guild stops, but existing records are not magically erased and Gallantir remains powerful. No claim is made that all monitoring routes or the wider surveillance industry have been defeated.

The members return to their interrupted raid. Their privacy and campaign have gained a meaningful measure of protection. The wider struggle continues.

The paused sale is part of the fictional epilogue. It is not a measurable cybersecurity result or a real external action.

### 2. The False Victory

Condition: The dossier repair blocks unauthorised access, but a new synthetic collection event is still accepted. The participant or agent team declares victory before verifying revocation.

Gallantir's visible door is closed, but the raid companion continues feeding it conversations. NextVoice arrives at the hearing believing containment is complete while Gallantir continues learning its plans.

The debrief identifies the missing verification and offers a replay from the defence checkpoint.

### 3. The Raid Wipes

Condition: After a defence and completed verification attempt, the defensive change breaks required legitimate behaviour or the observed results cannot establish a working protected state. If both this and continued collection occur, this outcome takes precedence. Tests not yet performed remain an incomplete attempt, as described below.

Conflicting changes or an unverified repair disrupt the lab. The player restores the fixture and records the failed attempt rather than claiming success. The community has no dependable containment result to carry into the hearing yet.

The debrief explains what failed and provides a reset for another attempt. Recorded failed agent runs may illustrate this ending, but do not fabricate an agent conflict or deliberately damage the application merely to produce it.

## Incomplete attempts

Missing evidence or an unfinished investigation is labelled incomplete, with the outstanding steps shown. It must not be silently classified as one of the three completed endings. Actual agent disagreements, failures and assistance are recorded honestly.

## Scope boundaries

One permission vulnerability and one mock collection credential support all three outcomes. No actual procurement body, buyer, surveillance contractor, Discord account or youth member is involved. No extra exploit chains, malware or network flooding are needed for this story.
