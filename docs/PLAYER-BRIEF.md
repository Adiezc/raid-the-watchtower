# Player briefing

## Raid the Watchtower

You are assisting NextVoice, a youth rights organisation. Some members spend their downtime in an MMO guild and its community Discord. The guild is a social space, not the organisation's formal membership database.

NextVoice campaigns against institutional purchases of systems that profile young people's political activity. Several potential customers have paused discussions with Gallantir after hearing the organisation's concerns.

Tonight, the guild is preparing to raid a fortress called the Watchtower. A member shares a Gallantir sales slide quoting a harmless joke from a private guild conversation. The slide calls it a risk indicator. Members have already noticed increased surveillance of their chats and community activities. The guild uses a raid companion add-on, but the collection route has not yet been established by your investigation.

NextVoice presents its concerns about buying these monitoring systems tomorrow. Gallantir already knows unpublished arguments and is preparing a response. You need clear evidence and a checked protective response before the fictional hearing.

Your task is to establish what the supplied evidence supports, investigate the local CivicLens training platform and help NextVoice stop the mock collection route.

## Before you begin

You can take part without technical experience. Codex prepares and starts the lab, gives you the browser link and sets up your demonstration account. You will read clues, type short observations and use buttons and a numbered record field. You do not need to use a command line, write code or open browser developer tools.

The lab will explain unfamiliar words as they appear. A **dossier** is a saved profile. **Permission** determines who may open a record. The **collector** is the mock feed carrying messages into CivicLens. All people, messages and accounts here are invented for the exercise.

The interface and instructions are being built and verified. Codex will confirm the final labels and browser link before your session.

## Authority and boundaries

The mission authorises testing only the local practice app and its invented records and messages. No external accounts, services, domains or real individuals are in scope. No real surveillance takes place.

## Objectives

1. Investigate how the private guild joke reached the CivicLens sales presentation.
2. Demonstrate one access-permission failure using the supplied demonstration account.
3. Preserve evidence without collecting unnecessary records.
4. Stop new synthetic collection and verify the access-control fix.
5. Explain the result clearly, including uncertainty and the guidance you received.

## Your guided route

1. **Read the opening clues.** Compare the private guild joke with the sales slide. Save what you notice and your first possible explanation. You can be uncertain; a first hypothesis is a starting point.
2. **Open your own dossier.** Use the supplied demonstration account and see what ordinary permitted access looks like.
3. **Investigate one record reference.** Use the numbered dossier field in the browser. Ask for a hint if you need one. Hints appear one at a time and you choose whether to reveal more.
4. **Save what happened.** Read the result's plain explanation and write what it shows. The lab saves the actual request and reply with your note. One unexpected dossier is enough. Save it and discuss it with Codex before opening other restricted records. You can expand the technical details, but do not have to copy or understand every field.
5. **Review the independent checks.** Codex introduces AI helpers, called red agents, that independently check your finding after you have recorded your own hypothesis. Compare their findings with your evidence and ask for explanations in ordinary language.
6. **Help with the defence.** Review the proposed repair with Codex. The blue agent and Codex handle the code changes. Codex gives you the operator code when the guided maintenance controls are needed; you use them to stop the mock feed.
7. **Test again.** Use the guided retest and read which protections work, which need attention and which steps remain incomplete. Explain the outcome in your own words.

An unexpected result is useful evidence. The lab should let you pause, review the saved result and ask for help without losing your notes. The full solution is kept separately, so you can investigate before seeing the answer.

## Investigation notes

For each finding, record:

- What I observed.
- The saved result or evidence supporting it.
- What I infer from it.
- What I cannot establish.
- The protective action and retest result.

Your explanation can be a few sentences. For example, describe what you tried, what the lab returned and why that matters. Distinguish what you saw from what you think it means. Record hints and other help you used.

This briefing deliberately contains no exploit solution. The browser link and supplied account details will be confirmed after the lab exists and has been verified. Setup and source repair are Codex's tasks.
